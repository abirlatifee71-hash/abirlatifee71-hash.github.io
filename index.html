Who I Am

My Story & Education

I'm Enamul Rais Latifee, a second-year student of Industrial & Production Engineering (IPE) at Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET) — the apex engineering institution in Bangladesh.

My journey is defined by a drive to lead, organize, and optimize. From heading debating clubs to coordinating large-scale events, I've consistently sought roles that blend technical rigor with human impact.

I'm passionate about how systems thinking, lean methodologies, and engineering principles can solve real-world industrial challenges. My goal is to shape a career at the intersection of technology, management, and innovation.

Beyond the classroom, I have arranged and co-organized numerous events — including শিল্পে গোলযোগ: IPE'24 Reception, বিতর্কের সাহিত্য (a National Debate Festival by CZSDC — Comilla Zilla School Debating Club), co-organized Hult Prize BUET 2026, TEDxBUET, and many other departmental and university events.
